# 📖 **RULEBOOK**

# Objective

Choose one mode:
**Last Cat Standing**
- Play until all other players go bankrupt
- The last remaining player wins
**Treasure Count**
- Stop at any agreed time
- Player with the most total dabloons (on-hand + stored) wins

# Components
20 Island Board
LCD Display System
LED strip Dice
Buttons: + / – / →
# Equipment
1 Gameboard
5 Cat playing figures
10 Event Cards
25 Chests (5 Per player)
5 Kit cards of dabloons (~1000 dabloons)


Setup
Turn on the system
Select number of players using:
+ (add player)
– (remove player)
→ (confirm)
Each player:
Starts with 20 dabloons
Places their token on Start Island
Shuffle the event card deck

Turn Structure
On your turn:
Press → to roll the LED dice
Move forward the shown number of spaces
Resolve the island you land on

Island Types
Treasure Islands (Normal Tiles)
When landing:
40% chance to gain 1–5 dabloons
If the island is empty:
You may buy a treasure chest
Chest Costs
10 → 30 → 50 → 80 → 100 (max)
Upgrades (your island only)
20 → 50 → 80 → 120 → 200 (max)

Owned Islands
Landing on another player’s island:
You must pay:
 5 → 10 → 20 → 40 → 60 dabloons
(depending on chest level)
Payment rules:
Must pay using on-hand dabloons
If you cannot pay → go to Prison

Event Islands
Draw a card and follow instructions
Return card to deck and reshuffle if needed

Prison
If you cannot pay:
You are sent to prison for 2 rounds
Skip your turns
Return after all other players take 2 turns

Start Island
Passing or landing:
Gain 15 dabloons

Dabloon Management
Storing
Only on your own islands
You may store any amount
Stored dabloons are safe from theft
Withdrawing
Only when landing on that specific island
Only from that island’s chest

Stealing Rules (Important!)
Landing on a Player
If you land on a tile occupied by another player:
You steal ALL dabloons they are carrying

Special Case: Island Owner Advantage
If you are on your own island:
You rob any player who lands there
They do not pay a fine (robbery replaces payment)

Chain Stealing
If multiple players are on the same tile:
The newest arriving player steals from the most recent holder of dabloons

Bankruptcy
A player is eliminated if:
They cannot pay a required amount
AND have no way to recover

🏁 End of Game
Triggered by:
One player remaining OR
Players agreeing to stop
Count all dabloons:
On-hand + stored
Highest total wins!



In this game, players take on the role of pirate cats sailing between islands, collecting dabloons, hiding treasure, and stealing from rivals.
Managing your wealth is key:
Carry too much → risk getting robbed
Store too much → risk being unable to pay
The richest (or last surviving) pirate cat wins!
